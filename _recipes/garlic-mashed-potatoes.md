## Ingredients

- 8 russet potatoes, peeled / cut
- 1 stick butter
- 20 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups whole milk
- Salt, to taste
- 1–2 cups shredded mozzarella

## Instructions

1. Boil potatoes until fork tender.
2. Drain and mash in a large bowl.
3. Blend in milk until smooth.
4. Melt butter with garlic; mix in.
5. Salt to taste.
6. Fold in mozzarella while hot.
